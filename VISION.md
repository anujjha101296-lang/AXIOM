# AXIOM Vision

## Why AXIOM exists

AXIOM builds systems that measurably increase humanity's ability to solve hard problems.

Scientific discovery, mathematics, engineering, and software are candidate domains—not permanent constraints. The company discovers the most valuable direction through evidence, customer learning, and reproducible technical progress.

## Execution model

AXIOM progresses on three connected tracks. None waits for the others to be “finished.”

| Track | Objective | Near-term evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Build the artificial-scientist capability stack. | Reproducible benchmarks, trustworthy verification, and research workflows. |
| Product | Build an AI workspace researchers actively use. | A clear MVP, observed user workflow value, and early research users. |
| Company | Make the work legible and reachable. | Honest website, documentation, pilots, user learning, and a credible operating cadence. |

The tracks share evidence and capabilities: product use exposes research needs, research advances create defensible product value, and company work tests whether the organization can sustain both.

## Product thesis

AXIOM's first public product is an AI workspace for frontier mathematical and scientific research. It begins close to real research and engineering work—through early-user workflows and, where useful, services—so that the team can create accountable outcomes and expose repeatable patterns. Repeated workflows may become software products; physical products are pursued only when evidence makes them the best vehicle for impact.

## Engineering thesis

The repository is a compounding capability platform, not a demo collection. Each subsystem must either:

1. improve the quality, speed, reliability, or cost of solving hard problems; or
2. make another useful capability safer, more verifiable, or easier to reuse.

The Mathematical Intelligence Platform is AXIOM's initial technical wedge. It supplies knowledge representation, formal verification, symbolic computation, research planning, and evaluation patterns that can later serve other domains.

## Decision standard

Mission, market, product, and technical direction are hypotheses. We strengthen or retire them based on ranked evidence:

1. observed customer outcomes, paid commitments, and production usage;
2. controlled experiments and reproducible benchmarks;
3. authoritative research and direct expert feedback;
4. internal prototypes and architectural analysis; and
5. model-generated inference.

Evidence must never be presented as stronger than it is. A simulated verifier is not a formal proof, a benchmark score is not customer value, and a roadmap is not a commitment.

## Near-term aim

Make AXIOM a reliable, measurable research-engineering platform: a system that can ingest knowledge, propose and test hypotheses, verify bounded claims, preserve provenance, and report what it actually knows.

## Long-term aim

Develop durable capabilities that allow AXIOM to take on increasingly consequential scientific and technical problems, including prize-backed open problems when the evidence supports readiness.
