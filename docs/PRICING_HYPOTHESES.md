# Pricing Experiment Hypotheses

## H1: The Prosumer SaaS Model
**Hypothesis**: Individual PhDs and solo researchers will pay out-of-pocket for a $20/month subscription if it guarantees rigorous verification of their hypotheses, mirroring the ChatGPT Plus baseline.
**Measurement**: Tracking conversion rates from FREE -> PRO after exhausting the 5 free monthly research runs.

## H2: The Team License Model
**Hypothesis**: Formal verification engineers and applied math research teams require shared project workspaces and shared organizational billing.
**Measurement**: Number of requests to upgrade from PRO to TEAM (monitored via click-tracking on the /settings/billing UI).

## H3: Value-Based Inference Pricing
**Hypothesis**: Users want a low base subscription but are willing to pay per-mission for deeper search horizons and more Z3 timeout allowances.
**Measurement**: Not yet implemented. Will observe if users consistently hit the 50-mission PRO cap.
