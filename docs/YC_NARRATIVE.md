# AXIOM YC Application & Founder Narrative

## 1. What is AXIOM?
AXIOM is an autonomous research operating system for discovering, testing, and verifying new scientific and mathematical knowledge.

## 2. Who uses it?
Quantitative researchers, computer scientists, university laboratories, and frontier AI research teams working on formal reasoning.

## 3. What painful problem exists?
Current AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity) hallucinate math and science because they operate purely as text generation engines. Researchers spend hours manually verifying LLM output.

## 4. Why existing AI tools are insufficient?
Generative LLMs do not execute code sandboxes, sweep finite counterexamples, or compile proof scripts against formal verification kernels (Lean 4 / Z3 SMT).

## 5. What does AXIOM uniquely do?
AXIOM connects AI reasoning to real Python execution sandboxes, Z3 SMT solvers, and Lean 4 kernel verification, ensuring that every claim is grounded and verified.

## 6. What is the strongest demo?
Taking a mathematical claim, running a 10,000,000-case sandboxed simulation, sweeping counterexamples in Z3 SMT, and compiling a sorry-free Lean 4 proof in under 2 seconds.

## 7. What evidence do we have?
114 passing backend tests, 16 prerendered frontend routes, 100% clean Next.js production build, and functional Lean 4 kernel verification.

## 8. What is the long-term vision?
To build the autonomous artificial scientist platform that accelerates mathematical, computational, and scientific discovery.

## 9. What is the moat?
The integrated epistemic knowledge graph, approach memory anti-rediscovery engine, and multi-layered formal verification pipeline.
