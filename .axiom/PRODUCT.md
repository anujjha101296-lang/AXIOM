# Product Contract

Read `CONSTITUTION.md`, `GTM.md`, `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md`, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, and `MEMORY.md` before product decisions.

## Product thesis

AXIOM starts close to real research and engineering work. A service engagement is a learning instrument: it exposes customer workflows, measures value, and identifies repeatable capabilities that may become products.

## Product rule

No feature is justified by novelty alone. It must state the user, job to be done, current alternative, measurable value, evidence level, risks, and smallest validation experiment. A prototype is not a product until it has a defined user, observed usage or validated demand, reliability expectations, and an owner.

## Portfolio decisions

Use `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md` to rank opportunities. Update `GTM.md` with customer and market evidence, `CAPABILITIES.md` with reusable technology, and `MEMORY.md` with rejected ideas and their reasons. Human approval is required for pricing, public positioning, contracts, and external commitments.
