## Role of prize problems

Prize-backed problems are long-horizon capability tests, not near-term delivery promises. AXIOM optimizes for the organization capable of making verified contributions—not for a score, demo, or unsupported claim of progress.

## Readiness standard

For each target problem, record prerequisites, benchmarked capability levels, proof/verification status, literature evidence, independent review, known gaps, and reproducible artifacts. A readiness score is an internal hypothesis unless grounded in recorded measurement; it must expose confidence and limitations.

## Current focus

Mathematical intelligence is the active initial wedge, including analytic number theory and Riemann-Hypothesis-adjacent tooling.
