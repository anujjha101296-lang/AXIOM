# Go-to-Market Contract

Read `CONSTITUTION.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md`, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, and `MEMORY.md` before go-to-market work.

## Initial approach

Begin with evidence-led research and engineering services where AXIOM can create verifiable value. Turn repeated, high-value workflows into product hypotheses only after observing the work in context.

## Evidence required

For each segment, capture the problem, workflow, current alternative, buyer, urgency, willingness to pay, security and procurement constraints, and evidence source. Rank direct interviews, paid pilots, and production use above desk research and model inference.

## Boundaries

AI workers may prepare research plans and internal materials. Humans must approve external outreach, marketing claims, pricing, contracts, data processing agreements, and use of customer information. Link customer insights to `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`, decisions to `MEMORY.md`, and validated needs to `PRODUCT.md` and `TASK_QUEUE.md`.
