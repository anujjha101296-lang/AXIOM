# Research Contract

Read `CONSTITUTION.md`, `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md`, `CAPABILITIES.md`, `PRIZE_TRACK.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`, and `MEMORY.md` before research work.

## Research unit

Every research activity is a bounded unit with: question, hypothesis, prior evidence, method, inputs, expected signal, success threshold, stop condition, owner, and artifact location. An unanswered question is not evidence; an unsuccessful result is retained as evidence about the method or hypothesis.

## Evidence standards

Record provenance, tool/runtime versions, datasets or papers, assumptions, confidence, and reproducibility instructions. Distinguish:

- observation;
- model-generated proposal;
- benchmark result;
- independently reproduced result;
- formal verification; and
- human/expert review.

Do not claim novelty, discovery, or prize relevance without a traceable evidence record and appropriate independent review.

## Research portfolio

Balance exploitation of high-signal capabilities with exploratory bets. Prefer small experiments that reduce decisive uncertainty. Promote a result into `CAPABILITIES.md` only when it is repeatable and measurable; log all outcomes in `MEMORY.md` and link evidence in `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`.
