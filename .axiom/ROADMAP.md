## Track A — Research (continuous)

### Horizon 0 — Trustworthy baseline

Establish a supported Python 3.10+ runtime, achieve a reproducible test baseline, audit verification-status truthfulness, and integrate EPIC-002 evaluation only with evidence-backed tests.

### Horizon 1 — Measurable research capability

Make ingest, knowledge representation, bounded hypothesis generation, counterexample search, formal verification, and evaluation reproducible with explicit provenance and metrics.

## Track B — Product (continuous)

### Milestone 1 — Researcher workflow MVP (1–2 months)

Deliver one end-to-end workflow that a mathematical/scientific researcher can understand, try, and evaluate. Its value proposition, limits, and supporting documentation must be honest and testable. Target learning signal: 10–20 early users or equivalent structured research-user conversations; no user count is claimed until measured.

### Milestone 2 — Public alpha and pilots (3–6 months)

Operate a public alpha with feedback instrumentation, a strong technical demo, and narrowly scoped institutional/research pilots if humans authorize outreach. Target learning signal: repeat use, measured time saved or insight quality, and a credible case for an accelerator/funding application.

### Milestone 3 — Validated research platform (6–12 months)

Grow adoption only with reliable core workflows and documented outcomes. Target learning signal: original, reproducible research features and independently reviewed technical validation or publication.

## Track C — Company (continuous)

### Foundation — Public clarity and operating cadence (weeks 1–6)

Maintain an honest landing page, product documentation, demos/screenshots as features stabilize, a research roadmap, benchmark evidence, and weekly PMO reviews. Company work exists to test product clarity and learn from the market—not to overstate capability.

### Horizon 2 — Evidence-led service learning

Use validated research/engineering engagements to identify recurring, valuable workflows and convert only the strongest patterns into product hypotheses.

### Horizon 3 — Compounding discovery organization

Expand domain capabilities and pursue prize-adjacent work only when independent evidence shows prerequisite capability, verification, and organizational maturity.

## Evolution rule

At every completed cycle of the Continuous Evolution Loop, retain completed outcomes, remove disproven assumptions, revise priorities using `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md`, and update `CURRENT_STATE.md`, `TASK_QUEUE.md`, `CAPABILITIES.md`, and `MEMORY.md`. A roadmap item without an evidence source, owner, or measurable next step is not actionable.

Quarterly objectives are set in the monthly strategic review (Layer 1). Engineering work (Layer 2) advances objectives only through measured capability improvement — not feature count.
