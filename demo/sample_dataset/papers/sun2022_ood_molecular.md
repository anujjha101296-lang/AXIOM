# Sun et al. 2022 — Out-of-Distribution Generalization on Molecular Graphs

## Abstract (demo excerpt)

We benchmark GNN generalization under scaffold-based train/test splits. Standard models exhibit large performance drops when test molecules have scaffolds absent from training data.

## Key concepts for extraction

- Scaffold split evaluation
- Out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization
- Substructure frequency
- Invariant risk minimization (IRM)

## Findings

1. Scaffold splits reduce AUC by 15–30% vs random splits
2. Rare substructures correlate with higher prediction error
3. Pre-training alone does not eliminate scaffold OOD gaps
